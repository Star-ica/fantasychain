Executive Summary

FantasyChain is a fully decentralized fantasy sports protocol deployed on BOT Chain (Chain ID 677). It enables anyone to create or join competitions, build teams from real-world player rosters, have performance scores submitted by trusted oracles, and claim prizes from escrow contracts — all without a central authority. The protocol is composed of six interdependent smart contracts: CompetitionFactory, PlayerRegistry, TeamManager, ScoringManager, PrizeEscrow, and ClaimManager.

Contract Architecture

CompetitionFactory — Creates and manages competitions with entry fees, prize pools, deadlines, and oracle assignments.
PlayerRegistry — Admin-managed registry of real-world athletes with positions, teams, and status updates.
TeamManager — Handles team creation, entry fee collection, and player validation per competition.
ScoringManager — Oracle-submitted match results with 48-hour dispute windows and score aggregation.
PrizeEscrow — Custodian of prize funds with emergency withdrawal and ClaimManager release mechanism.
ClaimManager — Distributes prizes (50/30/20 default) to ranked winners with reentrancy protection.
